Why we're doing this

They're ten. The scoreboard can wait.

Nobody knows who the best players will be at 14. What helps most at this age is good coaching, plenty of puck touches, and teammates they're excited to see at the rink.

So the goal is to get good kids and good families together now, and build bonds that carry into future house league seasons and onto travel teams down the road.

Who it's for

The culture comes first

We're building this group around how kids play and how families show up, more than around the stat sheet.

  • Effort every shift

    Kids who work just as hard when the game isn't going their way.

  • Coachable

    Kids who listen, try what they're taught, and want to learn.

  • Good teammates

    Kids who share the puck and are happy when someone else scores.

  • Good families

    Parents who cheer for every kid on the bench and keep it fun.

You're receiving this invitation because we think your player and your family fit that culture.
